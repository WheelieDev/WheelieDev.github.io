Mobile Game Privacy Policy

Policy version: 15 November 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy is provided by Tom Easterbrook, an individual based in the United Kingdom ("I", "my" or "me"), for my apps available on the Apple App Store, for iPhone, iPad and Mac (the "Apps").

This policy explains what personal data the Apps collect, how and why I use it, who I share it with, and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR). I am the controller of personal data collected through the Apps.

What this policy applies to

This policy covers your use of the Apps only. The Apps use services provided by Apple and Google, which have their own privacy policies that I don't control.

Where adverts appear

Adverts only appear in the free versions of the Apps on iPhone and iPad. If you buy the one-off Remove Ads purchase, the App stops showing adverts and no longer starts Google's advertising service, so the advertising data described below is not collected from then on.

Paid versions of the Apps, including the Mac versions, have no adverts and do not use Google AdMob. They don't collect any personal data.

Personal data collected

  • Data you provide: If you contact me, your name, email address and the content of your message.
  • Data stored in the App: Content or settings you enter into an App are stored only on your device. I cannot see them.
  • Advertising data: The free iPhone and iPad versions of the Apps show adverts provided by Google AdMob, unless you've bought Remove Ads. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and operating system, and information about how you interact with adverts. With your consent, this is used to show personalised adverts. Without it, you will see non-personalised adverts, which don't use your advertising identifier for tracking. You can read how Google uses this information here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Purchase data: The free iPhone and iPad versions offer a one-off payment to remove adverts, and some Apps (such as the Mac versions) are paid for up front. Apple handles all payments through the App Store. I do not receive or store your payment card or billing details. I only receive confirmation from Apple that a purchase has been made, so the App can remove adverts.

I do not collect your location. The Apps are not designed for children, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Your choices about advertising

  • Consent: When you first open an App, you'll be asked whether you agree to personalised advertising. You can change your choice later in the App's settings.
  • Apple's tracking prompt: On iOS, the App will ask your permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Device settings: You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalisation in your device settings.
  • Remove adverts: You can pay a one-off fee in the App to remove adverts. This also stops the advertising data collection described above.

Who I share your data with

  • Google (AdMob), who receives the advertising data described above to show and measure adverts. Google acts as an independent controller under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple, who processes purchases and provides the App Store.
  • Law enforcement, courts and regulators, where required by law.

I do not sell your personal data in exchange for money, and I do not share it with anyone else for their own marketing.

Transfers outside the UK

Google and Apple process data outside the UK, including in the US. Where this happens, they rely on a UK adequacy regulation (including the UK–US data bridge where the provider is certified), the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or another safeguard permitted by UK law.

How long I keep your data

  • Data stored on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, and for up to 12 months afterwards, then deleted.
  • Records of purchases and income are kept for 6 years to meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Data held by Google or Apple is kept under their own retention policies.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access a copy of your personal data
  • have mistakes corrected
  • have your data erased, in certain situations
  • restrict how I use your data, in certain situations
  • receive your data in a portable format, in certain situations
  • object to my use of your data where I rely on legitimate interests
  • withdraw consent at any time, including for personalised adverts

To exercise any of these rights, email me at the address below with enough information to identify you. I'll respond within one month. There is normally no charge. For data that Google holds as an independent controller, you may also need to contact Google directly.

More information is on the ICO website: https://ico.org.uk

Keeping your data secure

I use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, I will notify you and the ICO where I am legally required to. For advice on protecting yourself online, visit www.getsafeonline.org.

How to complain

Please contact me first so I can try to resolve any concern. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. For significant changes, I'll notify you through the App or by another suitable method.

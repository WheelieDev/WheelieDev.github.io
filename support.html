Solitaire: Classic Edition

  • Remove Ads: on iPhone and iPad, open the pause menu (the Menu button, bottom right) and tap Remove ads. It's a one-off purchase. The Mac version has no adverts.
  • Bought Remove Ads before, on a new device? Open the pause menu and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Undo: tap Undo (or press ⌘Z on the Mac) to take back moves one at a time, as many as you like.
  • Hints: tap Hint (or press H on the Mac). The first hint points you along a way to win. Tap again to see other moves.
  • Can every game be won? Yes. Every deal is checked before it's dealt, so there's always a way through.
  • Advertising choices: in the UK and EU, open the pause menu and tap Privacy settings to change what you agreed to. See the privacy policy for more.
